Chosen company: **Broadcom Inc.**

Why I chose Broadcom
Broadcom is the strongest overall investment candidate among the three because it offers the best blend of **AI infrastructure exposure, durable competitive advantages, and diversified revenue streams**.

Key reasons:
1. **Best position in the AI infrastructure buildout**
   - Broadcom benefits from AI demand across **custom AI silicon**, **high-performance networking**, and **enterprise infrastructure software**.
   - This gives it multiple ways to monetize AI rather than relying on a single product category.

2. **More diversified and resilient than the other candidates**
   - Compared with AMD, Broadcom has a more balanced business model: semiconductors plus recurring software revenue.
   - Compared with Palantir, Broadcom is less dependent on premium valuation and contract-driven sentiment.
   - That makes Broadcom a higher-quality core holding for investment.

3. **Strong recent operating momentum**
   - Research indicates Broadcom’s **fiscal Q3 2026 results** showed very strong demand for custom AI accelerators and networking.
   - AI semiconductor revenue grew sharply year over year, which supports the view that the company is already converting AI demand into meaningful revenue.

4. **Strategic expansion beyond chips**
   - The **VMware Private AI Cloud** initiative broadens Broadcom’s opportunity into secure enterprise AI infrastructure.
   - This matters because it expands the company’s addressable market and strengthens its enterprise AI narrative.

5. **Durable moat and customer relevance**
   - Broadcom’s combination of custom accelerators, networking, and software creates a stronger moat than a pure-play chip or software company.
   - It is well positioned to benefit from sustained hyperscaler spending and enterprise AI adoption.

6. **Best risk/reward balance**
   - Broadcom still carries risks, especially valuation sensitivity and potential slowdown in hyperscaler capex.
   - But relative to the other options, it offers the most convincing mix of growth, quality, and long-term durability.

Why the other companies were not selected

## Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.
AMD is a strong company and a credible AI infrastructure contender, but I did not choose it because it remains a **challenger rather than a leader** in this investment set.

Why it was not selected:
- Its AI position is improving, but it is still more dependent on proving execution against dominant competitors.
- The company has attractive upside, especially with EPYC CPUs, Instinct GPUs, and the Helios rack-scale platform, but much of the story is still **future potential** rather than already-established monetization.
- The investment case is more volatile and execution-sensitive than Broadcom’s.
- AMD looks like a promising higher-beta name, but not the most reliable choice for this investment decision.

## Palantir Technologies Inc.
Palantir has strong AI software relevance and defense traction, but it was not selected because it is **more valuation-sensitive and more dependent on market enthusiasm** than Broadcom.

Why it was not selected:
- Palantir’s business quality is strong, especially in defense and mission-critical workflows.
- However, the stock is highly sensitive to expectations and valuation, which increases risk for an investment choice.
- Its growth story is compelling, but the upside depends heavily on continued high multiples and sustained adoption.
- Compared with Broadcom, Palantir has less diversification and a more speculative risk profile.

Final decision
**Broadcom Inc. is the best company for investment** because it combines:
- strong AI infrastructure exposure,
- proven revenue momentum,
- diversified business lines,
- and a more durable long-term moat than AMD or Palantir.

It is the most balanced and investable choice among the three.
