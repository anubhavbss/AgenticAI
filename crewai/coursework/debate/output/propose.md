Yes — AI should run in a controlled way.

AI is powerful, but without control, power becomes risk. A controlled approach ensures AI is used safely, ethically, and for the benefit of society. It helps prevent misuse such as fraud, surveillance abuse, harmful misinformation, job disruption without preparation, and dangerous autonomous decisions.

Control does not mean stopping innovation. It means guiding innovation responsibly. Just as cars, medicine, and aircraft are regulated to protect people while still allowing progress, AI also needs rules, oversight, and accountability. With proper control, we can maximize AI’s benefits—better healthcare, faster research, improved education, and greater efficiency—while minimizing its dangers.

In short, AI should absolutely run in a controlled way because technology without responsibility is a threat, but technology with control becomes a tool for human progress.
